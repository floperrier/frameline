# A Story may be open to copying

Decided on 2026-10-11, in a design session held before any issue. Amends
`docs/adr/0067-a-story-is-copied-whole.md`.

0067 lets an Author copy their own Stories and nobody else's. Authors also learn
from each other, and a branching work is learned by taking it apart, which is
what the Sample is for. A copy, though, takes everything: Images, Sounds and words
that another person made. **So another Author may copy a published Story only
when its own Author has made it Open to Copying**, which they do Story by Story
and which is off by default. Letting anyone copy any Listed Story was refused:
being found is not the same as being given away.

**What is copied is the Edition**: what Readers read, never the Story as its
Author is still writing it, which stays theirs alone until they publish it
(`docs/adr/0069-a-published-story-is-read-as-it-was-published.md`). Otherwise the
copy is made as 0067 makes it: whole, unpublished, unlisted, belonging to whoever
made it, and with no Comments, Lists or counts.

**The copy carries a Credit for good.** The Credit reads *after "Title" by Name*,
on the copy's title card and wherever the copy is presented, and its new Author
cannot remove it. It records the Story copied and that Story's Author as they
stood when the copy was made. A Story renamed, unpublished or deleted since still
keeps its Credit, without a link once the original can no longer be read. A copy
of a copy credits the Story it was copied from and not the chain before it.

**Withdrawing Open to Copying stops future copies, never the ones already made.**
A copy is a Story of its own from the moment it exists.

## Consequences

**A copy is a Notification** for the Author of the Story copied
(`docs/adr/0089-a-notification-reaches-an-author-three-ways.md`).
